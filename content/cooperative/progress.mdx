Last 30 days runs from 18 October to 16 November 2026, and past 12 months from 16 November 2025. Visitors are unique visitors per window from Vercel Web Analytics, so they can't be added up across months. Email counts are threads, dated by their first message. hello@ counts all inbound mail, not only support requests, and support@ counts only the threads that also reached Josh's inbox.

 Entwickelt von der Swiss AI Initiative rund um EPFL, ETH Zürich und das Supercomputing-Zentrum CSCS.

## In der Schweiz trainiert

## From Swiss research

Developed by the Swiss AI Initiative around EPFL, ETH Zurich and the CSCS supercomputing centre

## Fully open

Model weights, training recipe and data basis are openly accessible and auditable

## Multilingual and built for Switzerland

Apertus was trained on more than 1,800 languages, around ten times more than comparable language models. This is less about translation quality than about multicultural representation: many cultures are reflected in the training data, including all of Switzerland's national languages.

## Trained in Switzerland

Trained on the Alps supercomputer at CSCS in Lugano
